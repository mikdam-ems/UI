Seventy-four steps, one pair of hands.

Every Sotto is built start to finish by one maker, who signs the inside of the runner.

  1. I

    Ribs

    Steel ribs, filed and tipped with brass so they never snag the cloth.

  2. II

    Canopy

    Waxed cotton cut in panels, sewn tight, then stretched for a night.

  3. III

    Handle

    Chestnut, malacca or ash, steamed and bent by hand into a crook.
